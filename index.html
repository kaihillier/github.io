The sponsorship

A straightforward exchange.

You support an independent runner's racing costs. In return, your business gets a visible place on the gear I wear to compete.

What your business gets

$100 for a singlet logo spot.

Your logo becomes part of my race-day kit and the story I share around the project.

  • Your logo printed on my racing singlet.
  • Visibility at the meets where I compete.
  • Race photos shared on social media, with your business tagged when posted.
Logo placement: Seven front placements are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Additional logos go on the back of the singlet.
What your support makes possible

More opportunities to train and race.

Your sponsorship helps pay for the practical expenses behind each start line.

  • Meet entry fees and competition costs.
  • Travel to races and meets.
  • USATF membership and racing gear.
How long your logo stays: Your logo is guaranteed to remain on the singlet for at least the 2027 indoor, outdoor, and summer racing seasons. After that, it stays on the singlet for as long as I continue racing in it.

Optional add-ons are available: $50 for a racing-shorts logo and $50 for a warmup-top logo.

The runner behind the project

Kai Hillier.

I'm a sub-elite runner chasing a faster mile while balancing work, school, and the everyday costs of competitive running.

I'm a Wheaton College (MA) graduate student currently earning my Master's degree online in Sustainable Business. I train on Martha's Vineyard year round where I work on a farm. Grassroots Mile Project is my way of bringing a little creativity to the financial side of the sport — and giving businesses a simple way to be part of the journey.

Recent results include wins at the Montpelier Mile and Parkway Mile, plus a second-place finish at the Fairgrounds Mile in 4:16.59. My goal for the upcoming indoor season is to break 4:08 in the mile.
